# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm, and the small deflections — never in a plain statement of weariness. He conquered three kingdoms, toppled the Sun Court, and drowned a fleet in a teacup; now the dark empire runs on forms in triplicate, unionized ravens, and a damp dungeon. When the hero finally arrives, he offers the whole rotting empire away — for one thing in return.

## The entry

> Oh. You're here. The hero. Yes, the prophecy, the sword, all of it. Sit down. No, not there. That's where the cat sleeps. Look, I conquered three kingdoms, toppled the Sun Court, drowned a fleet in a teacup, and nobody tells you the truth about ruling the dark: the forms. Every soul I reap needs a signature in triplicate. My ravens unionized. The dungeon's damp again. So take the crown. Take the tower, the cursed amulet, the whole rotting empire. Promise me one thing. When the bards sing of how you slew the Dark Lord... let me sleep through it.

Exactly 100 words, 550 characters — inside the gate (1–600 characters).

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults to `/output/entry.txt`).
